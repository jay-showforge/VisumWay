VisumWay v0.2.9 fixes listener recovery and adds a real Linux acceptance
record.

## Linux results

The Ubuntu 24.04/WSL2 test used real X11 capture, D-Bus notifications,
systemd, and native signed-in Codex. It exercised sampled desktop watches,
second-agent reuse, a genuine process crash collected by the journal timer,
a model-written Dunst alert, and opening the saved conversation in xterm.
Distribution restart and uninstall passed.

[Test environment and results](https://github.com/jay-showforge/VisumWay/blob/v0.2.9/docs/LINUX_VALIDATION.md)

## Listener fix

A fresh heartbeat from a stopped listener could delay its replacement.
The listener now relies on its exclusive OS lock. A regression test and
the real Linux service-restart test cover this case.
